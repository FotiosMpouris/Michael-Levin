# Companies tracker — Levin lab ecosystem

> **Research, not investment advice.** This page tracks organizations publicly tied to Michael Levin and his lab so we can follow how the science is being commercialized. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy, sell or invest. Private-company funding figures come from company statements and third-party databases and may be incomplete. Every link was checked on 2026-10-15.

**Status at a glance (2026-10-15):** none of the companies below is publicly traded. There is no public stock that gives direct exposure to Levin's work today. Paths that exist in principle are private (venture funds, private rounds, usually for accredited investors) — see "Ways to get exposure" at the bottom.

Levin himself discloses licensing: in [[2026-09-22-life-machines-synthbiosis|Life, Machines, and a Foundation for Synthbiosis]] he mentions three disclosures of companies that licensed the lab's technology, and in [[2026-09-21-biology-ai-diverse-intelligence|Biology, AI, and Diverse Intelligence]] he says "There's four companies that have licensed some of the work." His lab site lists Unravel Biosciences, Morphoceuticals and Fauna Systems among his affiliations ([drmichaellevin.org](https://drmichaellevin.org/)).

## Ways to get exposure (research notes, not advice)

- **No public market route today.** None of these companies trades on an exchange. Watch for: an IPO, an acquisition by a public pharma company, or a public company licensing Tufts bioelectric patents.
- **Private rounds.** Morphoceuticals has said publicly it is raising. Private rounds are generally limited to accredited investors and are high-risk and illiquid.
- **Venture funds.** Astonishing Labs and Prime Movers Lab are the funds closest to Levin's work; LP access is typically restricted.
- **Equity crowdfunding.** None of these companies has a public Reg CF campaign that we found as of 2026-10-15. Re-check quarterly.
