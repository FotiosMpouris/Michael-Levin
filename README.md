# Michael Levin Research Wiki

A research wiki on Michael Levin (Tufts) and his lab: bioelectricity, regeneration, cognition in cells, xenobots and anthrobots, and the Platonic-space hypothesis.

- `index.md`: start here. Also `whats-new.md`, `vision.md`, `companies/companies-tracker.md`
- `videos/`: one page per video from https://www.youtube.com/@drmichaellevin (date, link, plain-language ideas, word-for-word caption quotes)
- `topics/`: topic hubs that link the videos together
- `concepts/`, `entities/`, `queries/`: deeper synthesis pages (see `SCHEMA.md`)
- `raw/transcripts/`: caption transcripts used as sources
- `site/build.py`: builds the public static site into `site/dist/` (published on here.now)

Companies content is research, not investment advice.
