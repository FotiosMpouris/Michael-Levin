Michael Levin

Evidence labels used on this page - Confirmed — stated on his lab site or in a peer-reviewed paper (DOI given). Checked 2026-10-16. - His words — what he says in talks or interviews. Accurate as a report of his view; not proof the view is right. - Our reading — this wiki's interpretation.

Who he is (Confirmed)

  • Vannevar Bush Professor and Distinguished Professor, Department of Biology, Tufts University (lab site, PI page).
  • Director of the Allen Discovery Center at Tufts and of the Tufts Center for Regenerative and Developmental Biology (PI page).
  • Training lineage listed on his PI page: undergraduate research with Susan Ernst, PhD with Cliff Tabin, postdoc with Mark Mercola.
  • Journals: co-editor-in-chief of Bioelectricity; founding associate editor of Collective Intelligence (lab site).
  • Affiliations listed on his lab site include the Wyss Institute (Harvard), Santa Fe Institute, the Tufts/UVM Institute for Computer-Designed Organisms, and the companies Unravel Biosciences, Morphoceuticals, Fauna Systems and Astonishing Labs. See Companies tracker.
  • ORCID: 0000-0001-7292-8084.

What his lab says it studies (Confirmed, lab site)

The lab's stated theme is "Embodied Minds: understanding diverse intelligence in evolved, designed, and hybrid complex systems." Its main model system is morphogenesis, "the ability of multicellular bodies to self-assemble, repair, and improvise novel solutions to anatomical goals," and one specialty is developmental bioelectricity: how "all cells connect in somatic electrical networks that store, process, and act on information to control large-scale body structure" (drmichaellevin.org).

His core ideas (His words; see the topic pages for quotes)

  • Mind and intelligence sit on a continuum from single cells to whole organisms, not only in brains. See Cognition in cells (basal and diverse intelligence) and Basal Cognition.
  • Bioelectric patterns act like a body's working memory of its target shape; changing them can change what cells build. See Bioelectricity and Bioelectric Pattern Language.
  • The "cognitive light cone": the size of the goals a system can pursue measures how much of a self it is. See Cognitive Lightcone.
  • A "Platonic space" of patterns that minds and bodies draw on. He presents this as a hypothesis. See Platonic Space (Biological Interpretation) and Platonic space and ingressing minds.
  • From the 2026-06-15 interview (transcript: raw/transcripts/2026-06-15-LCqJKGnRcbA.txt): he describes the lab as roughly three-quarters wet-lab work on regenerative-medicine applications. That is his description, not an audited figure.

How strong the claims are (Our reading)

  • Strong: bioelectric signals help control body patterning; xenobots and anthrobots exist and move; adult frogs regrew legs after the 2022 treatment. All peer-reviewed and published.
  • Early: claims about xenobot learning and memory, and about treating humans. No human treatment based on this work is approved. Don't overclaim xenobot cognition (see Query: What Are the Cognitive Capacities of Xenobots?).
  • Hypothesis: Platonic space and "mind everywhere" are philosophical frameworks. They're useful for asking questions, but they aren't settled science.
