## How guest discovery works (feed/refresh.py)

- Searches YouTube for `"Michael Levin"`, `Michael Levin interview`, `Michael Levin podcast`, `Michael Levin talk` and `Michael Levin bioelectricity`, sorted by upload date and limited to the last month, 40 results each.
- Keeps a video only if all of these hold: uploaded on or after the last guest check minus 3 days (`checked` in feed/guest-candidates.json; the first run uses the feed's `updated` date); "Michael/Mike/Dr./Prof. Levin" or "@drmichaellevin" in the title or description (plain "Levin" is not enough); at least 10 minutes long; not on his own channel (UC3pVafx6EZqXVI2V_Efu2uw) or a channel in SKIP_CHANNELS; no clip words in the title (shorts, clip, highlights, compilation, reupload, trailer, teaser, best of); a title that does not match an item already in the feed (reuploads); and a video id not already in the feed, videos/, raw/transcripts/, the pending list or the rejected list.
- Survivors go to `pending` in feed/guest-candidates.json with `match: title` or `match: description`. Nothing reaches feed/latest.json or the site until step 3b accepts it.
- Waits between YouTube requests and checks at most 30 candidates per run, because fast bursts get rate-limited.
