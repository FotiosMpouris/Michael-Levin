Where Does Intelligence Live? Michael Levin on Evolution, AI, and Platonic Space

  • Date published: 2026-10-20
  • Speakers: Michael Levin, interviewed on the 632nm podcast (part two)
  • Format: Interview

  • Topics: Platonic space

In one paragraph

Levin lays out his Platonic space hypothesis: a structured, non-physical space that holds mathematical truths and also higher-agency patterns, including kinds of minds. Bodies, embryos, robots and AIs are interfaces that let those patterns into the physical world.

He also says "that space" and "a structured space" for the Platonic space.

How the two spaces relate

Platonic space is where the patterns come from. Morphospace, in his words, is "the space of form and function", the world an agent can explore and that cells can "reach into new areas of". He does not say outright in this video that morphospace sits inside Platonic space. Calling morphospace a part of the larger space is our reading, based on his line that developmental biology studies "other patterns in that space".
