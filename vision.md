# Connections to Fotios's vision

Why this research matters for the work Fotios Mpouris is building. Each connection names the source so it can be checked. These are our interpretations, not Levin's claims.

## 1. Build for the future, not past patterns
Fotios's test is whether something would still make sense on the first Mars colony. Levin is asking the same kind of question of biology: not "what does a body look like" but "what can cells build when we change the goals?" Regeneration, xenobots and anthrobots are what medicine looks like when you stop assuming the body's current form is fixed. See [[regeneration]] and [[xenobots-and-anthrobots]].

## 2. Agents caring for agents
Fotios's view is that "agents taking care of agents" will become necessary as AI agents and humanoid robots arrive. Levin's whole frame is that bodies already are layered teams of agents with their own goals (cells, tissues, organs) and that care means communicating with those agents, not micromanaging them. His call for an "ethical synthbiosis" with cyborgs, hybrids and new beings is the same ethic at a larger scale. See [[cognition-in-cells]] and [[2026-10-09-neuroscience-beyond-neurons|Neuroscience Beyond Neurons]].

## 3. One bot per job, closing the loop
Mambot (a robot that hypothesizes, builds, observes and revises) is an existence proof of an AI agent running the full scientific loop. It is the research version of the one-agent-per-job workflow we run: the agent does the work, a human sets the goal and judges. See [[2026-09-22-life-machines-synthbiosis|Synthbiosis talk]].

## 4. A language layer over things that cannot talk
Levin: "we have a system that's very good at doing things and knowing things and not great at talking about it." His lab is trying to talk to gene networks in English. That is the same design problem as care technology: give people who cannot easily speak for themselves (or their caregivers) an interface that turns signals into plain language. See [[2026-09-03-bohorquez-serruya-information-across-levels|Bohórquez and Serruya]] and [[ai-and-biology]].

## 5. Mind, memory and the brain (care relevance)
The clinical cases in [[2026-10-11-mind-body-interface-levin-kofman|Mind-Body Interface]] — memory surviving brain remodeling, terminal lucidity — are relevant to dementia care. They are case reports, not proof, and should never be presented as treatments. See [[mind-body]].

## 6. Getting ahead of the commercial wave
Fotios expects Levin's work to become very large. The [[companies-tracker|Companies tracker]] follows spinouts, licensees and funders. As of 2026-10-15 none is public; the page is research, not advice.
