# Michael Levin

## Who he is (Confirmed)

- Vannevar Bush Professor and Distinguished Professor, Department of Biology, Tufts University ([lab site, PI page](https://drmichaellevin.org/people/)).
- Director of the **Allen Discovery Center at Tufts** and of the **Tufts Center for Regenerative and Developmental Biology** ([PI page](https://drmichaellevin.org/people/)).
- Training lineage listed on his PI page: undergraduate research with Susan Ernst, PhD with Cliff Tabin, postdoc with Mark Mercola.
- Journals: co-editor-in-chief of *Bioelectricity*; founding associate editor of *Collective Intelligence* ([lab site](https://drmichaellevin.org/)).
- Affiliations listed on his lab site include the Wyss Institute (Harvard), Santa Fe Institute, the Tufts/UVM Institute for Computer-Designed Organisms, and the companies Unravel Biosciences, Morphoceuticals, Fauna Systems and Astonishing Labs. See [[companies-tracker|Companies tracker]].
- ORCID: [0000-0001-7292-8084](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-7292-8084).

## What his lab says it studies (Confirmed, lab site)

The lab's stated theme is "Embodied Minds: understanding diverse intelligence in evolved, designed, and hybrid complex systems." Its main model system is morphogenesis, "the ability of multicellular bodies to self-assemble, repair, and improvise novel solutions to anatomical goals," and one specialty is developmental bioelectricity: how "all cells connect in somatic electrical networks that store, process, and act on information to control large-scale body structure" ([drmichaellevin.org](https://drmichaellevin.org/)).

## His core ideas (His words; see the topic pages for quotes)

- Mind and intelligence sit on a continuum from single cells to whole organisms, not only in brains. See [[cognition-in-cells]] and [[basal-cognition]].
- Bioelectric patterns act like a body's working memory of its target shape; changing them can change what cells build. See [[bioelectricity]] and [[bioelectric-pattern-language]].
- The "cognitive light cone": the size of the goals a system can pursue measures how much of a self it is. See [[cognitive-lightcone]].
- A "Platonic space" of patterns that minds and bodies draw on. He presents this as a hypothesis. See [[platonic-space]] and [[platonic-space-and-ingressing-minds]].
- From the 2026-06-15 interview (transcript: `raw/transcripts/2026-06-15-LCqJKGnRcbA.txt`): he describes the lab as roughly three-quarters wet-lab work on regenerative-medicine applications. That is his description, not an audited figure.

## How strong the claims are (Our reading)

- **Strong:** bioelectric signals help control body patterning; xenobots and anthrobots exist and move; adult frogs regrew legs after the 2022 treatment. All peer-reviewed and published.
- **Early:** claims about xenobot learning and memory, and about treating humans. No human treatment based on this work is approved. Don't overclaim xenobot cognition (see [[2026-06-15-xenobot-cognition]]).
- **Hypothesis:** Platonic space and "mind everywhere" are philosophical frameworks. They're useful for asking questions, but they aren't settled science.
