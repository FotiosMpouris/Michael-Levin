# Xenobots and anthrobots

Xenobots are self-assembling living constructs from frog cells; anthrobots form from adult human airway cells with no genetic editing. They move, gather, show memory-like signaling, and anthrobots can heal a scratch across human neurons. Mombot, a robot that builds and studies xenobots, closes the loop of experimental science in morphology. Commercial thread: Fauna Systems (biobots) and Anthrobots, Inc. (Astonishing Labs portfolio).
