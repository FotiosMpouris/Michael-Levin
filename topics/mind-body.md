# Mind, body and brain

Clinical and developmental cases where mind and brain do not line up as expected — hydrocephalus with normal cognition, memory surviving metamorphosis, language networks built after birth by conversation, gut cells guiding decisions. Taken together they argue for a flexible, multiscale interface between mind and body.
