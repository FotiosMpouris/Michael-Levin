# Cognition in cells (basal and diverse intelligence)

Intelligence as 'reaching the same goal by different means' applies below the brain: cells, tissues and even small gene-network models show memory, learning and problem solving. The cognitive light cone measures how large a goal a system can hold. The working rule is empirical: guess the problem space, guess the goal, then perturb and measure.
