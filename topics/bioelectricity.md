# Bioelectricity

Every cell has a voltage across its membrane, and cells link into electrical networks through gap junctions. Levin's lab treats these networks as the body's medium for storing and acting on anatomical goals — the same hardware brains use, but much older. The lab builds tools to read and rewrite these patterns, which is why regeneration and cancer reprogramming become engineering questions rather than genetic ones.
