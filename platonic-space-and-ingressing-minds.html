Platonic space and ingressing minds

Levin's most speculative and most discussed hypothesis: that patterns — from mathematical truths to kinds of minds — exist in a non-physical space and 'ingress' through bodies and machines, which act as interfaces. He presents it as a research program with experiments under way, not settled fact. One questioner in the Mind-Body Interface Q&A asked whether it is a category error; Levin answers with the sorting-algorithm and gene-network results.
