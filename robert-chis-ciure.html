Morphospace / Robert Chis-Ciure

Robert Chis-Ciure

Researcher who works with Michael Levin on diverse intelligence; in the 2026-11-18 roundtable he says he happens "to sometimes be a philosopher". The video description of the 2026-11-18 roundtable links his Wolfram Institute research profile (wolframinstitute.org), and the host introduces him as being from the University of Sussex. This page only records what the sources on this site say.

What he says he works on (his words, as captioned)

  • He has chaired Karl Friston's theoretical neurobiology group for two years and had been working with Levin for about a year and a half at the time of recording.
  • He and Levin are "actively developing the the story behind the platonic spaces".
  • A 2025 paper with Levin proposed a scale-free measure of intelligence as search efficiency: the log of the cost of a blind search divided by the cost of the biological search. He reports two to three orders of magnitude for amoebas and about 21 orders of magnitude for planarian head regeneration under barium, even with conservative estimates. He now calls the random-walk baseline "somewhat a problematic" choice.
  • The joint paper "Cognition All the Way Down 2.0: Neuroscience Beyond Neurons in the Diverse Intelligence Era" (Chis-Ciure and Levin) is linked in the video description (Synthese, Springer). In the roundtable he describes its working definition of intelligence, after William James, as reaching the same goal by different means, formalized as efficiency.
  • He is skeptical of "emergence" unless it is quantified and testable, and points to Lionel Barnett's work on dynamical independence.

Where he appears on this site

  • Agency & Intelligence Across Scales (Wandering Thoughts, 2026-11-18).

How this connects

His efficiency measure is one of the few attempts to put a number on basal cognition, and his current work with Levin is on Platonic Space (Biological Interpretation).
