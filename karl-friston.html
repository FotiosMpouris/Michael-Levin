Morphospace / Karl Friston

Karl Friston

Neuroscientist and theorist behind the free energy principle and active inference. His UCL page is linked in the video description of the 2026-11-18 roundtable (fil.ion.ucl.ac.uk/~karl). This page only records what he says in sources on this site; it is not a full profile.

Where he appears on this site

  • Agency & Intelligence Across Scales (Wandering Thoughts, 2026-11-18): a roundtable with Michael Levin and Robert Chis-Ciure.
  • Does a town have goals of its own? (Science Conversations, 2026-10-28): named in the host's first question, which the host says draws on Levin's conversation with Chris Fields, Karl Friston and others. Friston does not appear.

His position in the roundtable (his words, as captioned)

  • Self-organization is best explained by the boundary conditions a thing must obey to persist. He ties these to the Helmholtz-Hodge decomposition: a flow that resists dissipation plus a circular, non-dissipative flow that shows up as oscillations at many scales.
  • A slower, coarser higher scale (for example the genome) sets the allowed futures of a faster lower scale (for example the phenotype). From below, that context looks the same in past and future, which he offers as a physics reading of Levin's suggestion that xenobots seem prepared for a world they have never lived in.
  • Active inference can be read as a 21st-century version of the good regulator theorem: to act on a world, a system has to be a good model of it. He suggests this is one way to describe Levin's "ingression".
  • He asks whether, on Levin's accounting, everything is a free lunch, with "optimization" a story we tell about it.
  • He separates autonomous behavior from agency that plans, and says large language models currently act without intentions.

How this connects

Friston's physics route and Levin's Platonic Space (Biological Interpretation) hypothesis aim at the same question: where the competencies of new systems come from. Friston answers without any extra space; Levin keeps that option open and treats it as an engineering question. See also Basal Cognition and Cognitive Lightcone.
